Top of Lenoir

Lenoir Hall

Located near the Pit, this is UNC’s largest dining hall. It offers a wide variety of buffet-style meals, including international cuisine, vegetarian and vegan options, and themed food stations

Since we operate a commercial kitchen where ingredient substitutions, recipe revisions and cross-contact with allergens is possible, we cannot guarantee that any food item will be completely free of allergens. If you need assistance navigating the menu options available due to allergens or intolerances, please speak with the dietitian, chef, or food service manager on duty.
